Inside a dossier

What you get per country.

The instability score

A 0–100 composite blending a structural baseline with live events: battles and strikes, protests and riots, news pressure, economic stress and security incidents — plus capped climate, cyber and wildfire pressure on top. Countries in active war never score below the conflict floor — the number can't pretend a war away.

  • Rebuilt from source events every few minutes, cached ten
  • Score components shown, not hidden — see what's driving it

The AI brief

A short analyst-style brief generated from the country's current headlines and signals — with the cited articles listed under it, so you can check every claim against its source in one click.

  • Briefs cite their headlines — no unsourced claims
  • Runs on the same multi-tier AI chain as the rest of the app

Active signals, pinned

Everything currently live in that country — quakes, outages, conflict events, military activity, infrastructure alerts — listed and pinned to the map, each with its source.

Escalation watch

Hotspot pairs and strategic risk contributors update as events land, so a dossier isn't a snapshot — leave it open and it keeps up.
