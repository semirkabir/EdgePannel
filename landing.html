FAQ

Is EdgePannel really free?

Yes. Every map layer, every feed — free, with no signup and no trial clock. No ads either, and since there's no account, there's no user data to sell.

Where does the data come from?

572 curated public feeds and institutional sources: USGS, NOAA, GDACS, ECB, FRED, World Bank, OpenSky, AIS vessel streams, Cloudflare Radar, official government channels and major wire services. Every panel cites its sources.

Do I need an account?

No. The dashboard is fully usable anonymously. An optional sign-in exists only to sync your panel layout and watchlists across devices.

Is it open source?

No — EdgePannel is proprietary software offered as a hosted service. The scoring methodology is published in full, so the numbers stay auditable even though the code is not.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. It's an installable PWA that works offline-first, translated into 21 languages including right-to-left scripts.
