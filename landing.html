FAQ

Is EdgePannel really free?

The data is. Every map layer, every feed, every lens — free forever, with no signup and no trial clock. No ads either, and with no account there's no user data to sell. Paid tiers never gate the raw data; they add the things computed on top of it — AI analysis, alerting, and tracking & history. See what's paid.

Where does the data come from?

680+ curated public feeds and institutional sources: USGS, NOAA, GDACS, ECB, FRED, World Bank, OpenSky, AIS vessel streams, Cloudflare Radar, official government channels and major wire services. Every panel cites its sources.

Do I need an account?

No. The dashboard is fully usable anonymously. An optional sign-in exists only to sync your panel layout and watchlists across devices.

What are the six lenses?

World, Tech, Finance, Commodity, Conflicts and Happy — the same live engine with layers, feeds and panels retuned per focus. Each lens lives on its own subdomain and is one click away.

Can I self-host it?

Not currently — EdgePannel is proprietary software offered as a hosted service. The methodology behind every score is published in full, so you can audit how the numbers are produced even though the source isn't distributed. See the terms for what your use covers.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. It's an installable PWA that works offline-first, translated into 21 languages including right-to-left scripts.
