Pricing questions.

Is the data really free?

Yes. Every feed, map layer and lens — all 680+ sources — is free forever with no account. Paid tiers never gate the raw data; they add AI analysis, alerting, tracking and history computed on top of it.

What exactly do I pay for?

Three things: AI (summaries, Analyst chat, situation reports, premium models), alerts & monitoring (custom rules, real-time checks, webhooks, area monitors), and tracking & history (live object tracking, 30-day playback, export). Everything else stays free.

Can I switch or cancel anytime?

Yes. Subscriptions are month-to-month (or yearly for two months off) through Stripe, and you can upgrade, downgrade or cancel whenever you like. Canceling drops you back to the free Hobbyist tier — you never lose access to the map.

Do I need any API keys?

No. Every feed is maintained for you. Bring-your-own-keys is still supported if you'd rather use your own AI provider, but it's never required.

What's the difference between yearly and monthly?

Yearly billing is ten months' price for twelve months of access — two months free. Same features either way; flip the toggle above to compare.

Can I self-host it?

Not currently — EdgePannel is proprietary software offered as a hosted service. The free tier is a real product rather than a trial, and the scoring methodology is published in full so the numbers stay auditable.
