How lenses work

Not filters. Rebuilds.

A lens isn't a saved filter on top of a generic dashboard — each one is a separate build of the same engine with everything off-focus removed.

Layers retuned per lens

The World lens exposes the full layer tray. Tech swaps conflict layers for datacenters, AI labs and startup ecosystems; Commodity swaps them for mines, plants and trade routes. You never scroll past layers that don't belong to your job.

  • 60 map layers in the full build, curated subsets per lens
  • Layer state persists per lens — each remembers your setup

Feeds and panels follow

The 680+ feed catalog is re-weighted per lens: Finance leads with central banks and market wires, Conflicts with OSINT and defense sources, Happy with vetted good-news outlets. Panels rearrange to match.

  • News clustering and dedup run identically in every lens
  • Dead code for other lenses is stripped from the build entirely

One click to switch

Every lens lives on its own subdomain and cross-links to the rest. Follow a story from the Conflicts lens to Finance to see the market reaction, or unwind in Happy when you're done.

Free, all six

There's no "pro lens." All six are the same AGPL-3.0 codebase, free with no signup — pick per monitor, per mood, or per job.
